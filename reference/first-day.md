# The first day

What happens on the new developer's first Monday, in order. This is the whole day. The onboarder gives it a piece at a time.

## Before 8:00

- Vendor parking is the north lot, in the rows marked V.
- Come in through the main lobby. The security desk issues a vendor badge. Bring photo ID.
- Jane Vance, the new hire's mentor, meets them in the lobby at 8:00. If she is not there, the security desk calls up to the project room.

## 8:00 to 8:30: the walk round, before sitting down

- Jane walks the new hire round before they go near a desk. Project room 2B, room 2A next door where Ravi sits, the break room, the bathrooms, and the double doors to the client's billing floor.
- She introduces each person on the team by name and what they own. Nobody expects the names to stick the first time. They are in `whos-who.md`.
- Standup is at 8:15 in 2B. Ten minutes, standing. On day one the new hire is introduced and listens.

## 8:30 to 10:30: laptop and access, with Jane

- The laptop is on the desk, set up by Ravi Menon the Friday before. Beside it is a sealed envelope from him. It is opened with Jane, at the first sign-in. What is in it is listed in `tools-and-access.md`.
- Jane sits with the new hire for the first sign-in. The technical team is stretched across the whole site, so they are not pulled in unless something will not open.
- The order: sign in to the laptop and change the password, join the vendor wifi, connect the VPN, open email, open the project chat, sign in to DEV, open the project repository, open Foundations.
- How to know it worked, four checks:
  1. In DEV, open the practice account Jane names and look at its last bill.
  2. Send Jane a one-line message in the project chat and see her reply.
  3. Open `plan/where-we-are.md` in the repository.
  4. Open Foundations and see the title screen of module 1.
- If a step fails, tell Jane right then. Later in the day, paste the full error text into a chat message to her. A pasted error gets fixed faster than a described one, and she decides whether it needs Ravi.

## 10:30 to 12:00: a first look, then module 1

- Jane takes fifteen minutes to show the project repository: where the plan is, where the requirements are, where the client meeting notes are.
- One page to read today: `plan/where-we-are.md`. What is due, when, and who owns it.
- Then the new hire starts Foundations module 1.

## 12:00 to 1:00: welcome lunch

- At noon Jane, Dana the team lead, and whoever else on the team can get away come by the new hire's desk and take them to lunch in the cafeteria on the first floor.
- The lunch is on the team. The new hire does not pay.
- Anyone stuck in a client meeting misses it, so it may not be all six. It is a chance to meet people away from the screens, and the deadline is off the table for the hour.

## 1:00 to 4:15: Foundations

- Finish module 1 and watch module 2. Module 1 is how CIS is laid out. Module 2 is how a bill gets made.
- When module 1 ends, start module 2. Nobody needs to be told.
- If both are done before 4:15, open the billing design summary in the repository and start reading. It is this week's reading anyway. Do not start module 3: two a day is the pace.
- The new hire keeps a list of questions as they watch. One line per question, with where it came up and anything already tried.
- Owen Brandt and Leila Haddad, the two other people who started today, are watching the same two modules. Comparing notes with them is encouraged.

## 4:15 to 4:30: questions with Jane

- Jane sits down with the new hire at their desk and goes through the list. She answers what she can, and takes the rest to the person who owns it.
- Any answer that was not written down anywhere goes into `new-hire/questions-answered.md` in the repository before the new hire leaves. The next person gets it for free.

## 4:30 to 5:00

- Finish the notes. Shut down. The team leaves at 5:00.

## What a good first day looks like

Dana's list for a first day has three things on it:

1. Everything opens: email, the chat, DEV, the repository, Foundations.
2. The new hire has met the team and knows where things are on the floor.
3. Modules 1 and 2 are watched and there is a question list.

If all three are true at 5:00, the day went the way the team wanted.

## Not today

- No build work, no defects, no code changes.
- No need to read the billing design or the client meeting notes. That is for later in the week, unless both modules are done early.
- No access to TST or CNV, and no need for it.
- No questions to Harlan Valley's staff about the project. See `how-we-work.md`.
- No timesheet. That starts Friday.
