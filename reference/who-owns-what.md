# Who owns what

Where a new hire's question goes, and whose area it is. This file is the one home for the routing rule. Other files point here. People are in `whos-who.md`.

## The rule

- **Everything goes through Jane.** She is the new hire's mentor and their one point of contact. She answers, or she takes the question to the person who owns it and brings the answer back.
- The reason: everyone else is working to the trial conversion date, and that includes the technical team. One person absorbing a new hire's questions costs the project far less than six people each losing ten minutes.
- The table below says whose area each kind of question is. That is so the new hire can note it on the list and start to learn who does what. They still take it to Jane.

## The question list

- The new hire keeps one running list through the day and goes through it with Jane at 4:15. She comes to the new hire's desk.
- Each line has the question, where it came up, and what was already tried. If nothing was tried, the line says so.
- Whose area it is gets added when it is known. Jane does not expect the new hire to know that yet.
- The list is why people working to a date are interrupted once a day and not twenty times.

## The routes that do not go through Jane

There are five. Each covers exactly what is written beside it.

- **The other new starters, Owen Brandt and Leila Haddad.** Anything in a module that does not make sense, and anything about being new. If the three cannot work it out, it goes on the list.
- **Gwen Sato in HR, by email.** The terms of the job: pay, benefits, expenses, probation, and how many days of leave the new hire has. She is at head office and off the project.
- **The security desk in the lobby.** A lost or forgotten badge, arriving when nobody from the team is there to meet you, and getting a first aider when nobody from Ostrander can be found. They call up to the project room.
- **Dana Whitlock, when Jane cannot be reached.** Only for something that stops all work. See the next section.
- **Dana Whitlock, in a disagreement with Jane that the two of them could not settle.** See below.

## When Jane cannot be reached

- If Jane is out for the day, Dana says at standup who is covering for her.
- If Jane is in a meeting and something is broken, send her the message anyway, with the error text.
- If an hour passes with no reply and the new hire cannot work at all, they tell Dana in the project chat.
- If the laptop or the chat is the broken thing, walk to Jane's desk. If she is away, Dana's. If neither is in 2B, tell whoever is. Any teammate will get word to Jane, and this is the one time it is right to interrupt them.
- From home, with no chat: text Jane. Her mobile number and Dana's are handwritten on the card in the envelope.

## An emergency

- An emergency is a fire alarm, someone hurt, or someone in danger. Nothing else counts, however urgent it feels.
- None of it goes through Jane first, and none of it goes on the list. What to do is in `the-place.md`, under "If something goes wrong".
- This stands apart from the five routes above. Those are for questions.
- Tell Jane afterwards.
- A safety question the notes do not answer, such as where an extinguisher is, goes to Jane the next time she is at her desk. It does not wait for 4:15 and it does not go on the list.

## Hitting a wall

- More than half an hour on the same thing with no progress: stop and message Jane. Say what the wall is and what has been tried.
- It does not wait for 4:15.

## Disagreeing with Jane

- Raise it with Jane first, with reasons. A proposal review is a conversation and she expects to be argued with.
- If it is still stuck after that, it goes to Dana, and Jane is told that it is going. Nobody goes round her quietly.

## Kept out of this folder on purpose

These are not written here and the guide does not have them.

- **Phone numbers.** Jane's and Dana's are handwritten on that card. The rest are on the contact sheet pinned in the project chat.
- **Addresses for the environments and the repository.** Ravi sets them up as bookmarks on the laptop.
- **Pay dates, benefits, expense rules and probation.** Gwen Sato has them.
- **Customer data.** No real account, name or balance is in this folder.
- **Contract terms and prices.** Dana and Helen handle the contract.
