# Who's who

The people a new developer on the billing team will meet or hear about, and what each one owns.

Everything a new hire needs from these people goes through Jane, their mentor. This file is so the new hire knows who people are and whose area a question falls in. How questions travel is in `who-owns-what.md`.

No phone numbers are in this folder. Jane's and Dana's are handwritten on the card in the envelope. The rest are on the contact sheet pinned at the top of the project chat.

## The billing team (project room 2B)

Seven people, counting the new hire.

- **Jane Vance**, senior developer, and the new hire's mentor. Six years on CIS, third rollout. She is the one point of contact: every question goes to her, and she answers it or takes it to the right person. She reviews the new hire's changes. Her desk is the one next to theirs. Her own area is rate changes and time-of-use rates.
- **Dana Whitlock**, billing team lead, and the new hire's manager. Runs the team and its plan, and sits across the table from the client's billing manager in every design and status meeting. Approves timesheets and time off. She is in client meetings most of the day. Her desk is the one nearest the door of 2B.
- **Andre Castillo**, developer. Owns notices: the notice clock, duplicate notices and third-party copies. He is building the second third-party copy, the team's one piece of custom work, and it has to run in the second trial conversion.
- **Nadia Farouk**, developer. Owns solar accounts, budget billing and bulk bill corrections. She was the team's last new hire, eight months ago, and the first to write her questions down.
- **Walt Novak**, conversion analyst. Owns everything that gets carried over from the old system: accounts, open balances, three years of bill history. He is the most loaded person on the team until the trial conversion is done.
- **Joanne Pruitt**, business analyst. Owns the requirements, the client meeting notes, the test scripts, the reports and the new print file layout. She is the team's day-to-day voice to the client's billing floor.
- **The new hire**, developer. The seventh. The team asked for another developer after the first trial conversion.

## Ostrander people outside the team

- **Ravi Menon**, site technical lead. Serves every Ostrander team on this project. Owns laptops, accounts, email, the VPN, the vendor wifi and the environments. He sits in 2A, next door. He is stretched across the whole site, so access problems go to Jane first and she brings him in when it needs him.
- **Helen Strand**, project manager for the whole rollout. Owns the overall plan and reports to the client's steering committee. A new hire will see her at the Monday all-hands and rarely needs her.
- **Gwen Sato**, HR at Ostrander's head office. Owns the terms of the job: pay, benefits, expenses, probation and the leave balance. Questions about any of those go to her. She is not on site and not on the project. Her address is in the welcome email the new hire got before starting.
