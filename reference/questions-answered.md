# Questions answered: Nadia's first week

This is a copy of the opening of `new-hire/questions-answered.md` from the project repository. Nadia Farouk joined the billing team eight months ago and wrote down every question she got answered in her first week, with who answered. The team has kept the file going since. Only her first week is copied here.

The wording is hers.

## Monday

**Where are the bathrooms?**
Past the break room, on the left, before you get to the double doors. (Joanne)

**Is the coffee free?**
Yes, it's Harlan Valley's. We chip in for milk, there's a jar on top of the fridge. Leave the mugs that have names on them. (Andre)

**Where does everyone eat?**
Most people bring something. There's a cafeteria on the first floor, open 11:30 to 1:30, takes cards, vendors are allowed. On Thursdays a few of us walk to the deli on Mill Street. (Joanne)

**Do I need a parking permit?**
No. North lot, rows marked V, free. Do not park in the south lot. (Walt, who found out the hard way)

**How do I reach Jane if the chat is down, or I'm ill at home?**
Her mobile and Dana's are handwritten on the card in your envelope. Text first. Teams also works on your phone if you install it. (Jane)

## Tuesday

**What is the product actually called?**
Ostrander CIS. Everyone just says CIS. It stands for customer information system, which is what utilities call a billing system. (Jane)

**What is it written in?**
You mostly don't write it, you configure it. Rates and rules are set up in CIS's own designer. When configuration can't do something, extensions are written in Java, and the database is PostgreSQL. I was told I would not write any Java in my first month and that was true. (Jane)

**Where's the code?**
Git, on Ostrander's own server. Module 3 walks you through it and you get access when you finish that module. (Jane)

**What is the VPN, and does it work from home?**
It's the Ostrander VPN app, the blue shield in the menu bar. Same sign-in as the laptop. Yes, it works from home. (Ravi, via Jane)

**What time can I get in, and how late can I stay?**
Vendor badges open the lobby gates from 7:00 to 6:30 on weekdays. Outside that, security won't let a vendor up without someone from Harlan Valley. 2B is unlocked while anyone is in it. Last one out locks it and hangs the key on the hook inside 2A. (Ravi, via Jane)

**What happens if nobody is in the lobby to meet me?**
Tell the security desk you're with Ostrander billing. They have the project room's extension and they call up. This happened to me on my second day and it took four minutes. (Dana)

## Wednesday

**Who is my actual boss, Jane or Dana?**
Dana. She does my timesheet, my time off and the checkpoints. Jane is my mentor, which means she's who I go to for everything. If I'm unsure which of them something is for, it's Jane. (Jane)

**Where do the defects live?**
In Jira, project HVB. The list from the first trial conversion is the saved filter called "TC1 open". Read access comes in week two. (Walt, via Jane)

**Can I talk to the people on the billing floor at all?**
Yes. Say hello, talk about anything that isn't the project. Denise will ask how you're finding it. She asks everyone. Project questions go to Jane. (Joanne)

## Thursday

**How many days off do I get?**
Gwen in HR has the number. It's also on the offer letter. (Dana)

**Is Friday casual?**
No. Harlan Valley doesn't do casual Fridays, so we don't. (Joanne)

**Why is everyone tense on the first Tuesday of the month?**
The client's steering committee meets that morning. Dana and Helen both go. Don't try to book Dana before lunch that day. (Andre)

## Friday

**What do I put on the timesheet?**
One line. Project is "Harlan Valley Billing", task is "Onboarding" for the first week, eight hours a day. Jane did the first one with me. (Jane)

**What do I say at standup when it's my turn?**
From week two: one line on what you did yesterday, one on what you're doing today, and anything in your way. Under thirty seconds. (Jane)

**Can I wear headphones?**
At your desk, yes. (Jane)
