# The place

The team works on site, in the client's building. This is the map. Smaller things about the building are in Nadia's notes, `questions-answered.md`.

## Getting in

- The building is Harlan Valley Electric's operations center.
- Vendor parking is the north lot, rows marked V. It is free and needs no permit. The south lot is for Harlan Valley staff.
- The main lobby is the only way in for vendors. The security desk issues the badge on day one. Bring photo ID.
- The badge opens the lobby gates and the second floor doors. It is worn where it can be seen, all day.
- Vendor badges work from 7:00 to 6:30 on weekdays.
- A forgotten badge means a day pass from the security desk. Someone from the team has to come down and sign.
- If nobody from the team is in the lobby, the security desk calls up to the project room.

## Getting to the room

- The lifts are behind the security desk. Second floor, turn right out of the lift.
- **2A** is the first door. Ravi Menon and the technical team.
- **Project room 2B** is the second. The billing team: seven desks and a table. Standup happens here.
- **2C**, across the hall from 2B, is Ostrander's customer service team.
- It is one straight hall. 2A and 2B are on the right as you walk from the lifts, and 2C is on the left. Coming out of 2B, the lifts and the stairs are to the left, and the break room and bathrooms are to the right.
- **The break room** is further down the hall, past 2B and 2C. Coffee, a water cooler, a fridge, a microwave. It is shared with Harlan Valley's staff, and all of it is for vendors to use too. Put a name on anything left in the fridge.
- **The bathrooms** are just past the break room, on the left.
- **The billing floor** is through the double doors at the very end of the hall. It is Glenn Marsh's floor, and vendors go in when invited to a meeting.
- Client meetings are in the conference rooms beside the billing floor. Dana or Joanne books them.
- 2B has a lock and a key. There is no door code. It is unlocked while anyone is in it. The last person out locks it and hangs the key on the hook inside 2A, and the first one in takes it from there. The technical team is in 2A from 7:00.

## If something goes wrong

- **Someone is badly hurt or in danger: call 911 first.** The building is Harlan Valley Electric's operations center, and its street address is printed on the back of the vendor badge. Stay on the line: the dispatcher says what to do for the person until help arrives. Then tell anyone on the team. This never waits for Jane or for the list.
- **The fire alarm.** Leave what is on the desk and walk out by the stairs beside the lifts. Do not use the lifts. Outside, go to the north lot, rows marked V, where the team parks. Dana counts heads there, or Jane if Dana is not on site. Nobody goes back in until the security desk says so.
- **A first aid kit** is on the wall in the break room, beside the fridge. For anything it cannot handle, tell whoever is nearest in 2B. If 2B is empty, try 2A next door, and after that go down to the security desk. The security desk has the building's first aiders on call.
- **Feeling ill during the day.** Tell Jane and go home. If she cannot be found, tell Dana or anyone in 2B, and go. Nobody is expected to sit it out.
- **That is all the team has written.** Where the extinguishers and alarm points are, whether there is a defibrillator, and how someone who cannot use the stairs gets out are Harlan Valley's building rules, and they are not in these notes. Ask Jane to show you. That question does not wait for 4:15.

## The break room

- It is where a new hire first meets the people from the billing floor.
- Say hello and say who you are. They have seen a lot of vendor faces and they notice who is polite.
- Talk about anything except the project. Project questions for the floor go on the list for Jane.

## Hours

- 8:00 to 5:00, Monday to Friday, on site. The whole team keeps those hours until go-live. Nobody on this project works from home.
- Lunch is noon to 1:00, and the team stops for it at the same time.
- The hours match the billing floor's day. The team is in the building when the client is, so a question from the floor gets an answer the same day.
- Nobody on the team is expected to stay late in their first month.
- The team takes the same public holidays as Harlan Valley. The holiday calendar is pinned in the project chat, beside the contact sheet.

## What to wear

- Business casual, Fridays included. Harlan Valley's office staff dress that way and the team matches the client.
